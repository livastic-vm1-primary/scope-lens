    02 / HOW IT WORKS

    No cloud.
    No guessing.

    The boring but costly questions often fall through the cracks. ScopeLens combines transparent checks with optional private model inference.

    01

    Transparent first pass

    Eight visible topics. Each gap has a fixed, editable client question. The heuristic scan requires no downloads or API keys.

    02

    Open-weight second opinion

    SmolLM2-135M-Instruct runs with Transformers.js on your device after the model is downloaded. The generated questions are suggestions, not fact checks.

    03

    Take it to your workflow

    Export Markdown notes, revise the scope with your customer, then use your own quoting or invoicing process. We store no brief on our servers.
